2025年10月27日

On October 27, 2025, an innovation practice activity under the theme "Creating Business Value Through ESG Innovation" was successfully concluded at the Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS). The event was co-organized by the course "Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Sustainability Assessment," taught by Assistant Professor Jong Mui Choo from the Institute of Environment and Ecology (iEE), and Honor Device Co., Ltd. It was attended by iEE Associate Head Zhang Zhenghua, with co-organization support from the Deep-Ring Shuo 51 Party Branch and the Student Carbon Neutrality Association.
